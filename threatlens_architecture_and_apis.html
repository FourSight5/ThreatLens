3. AI Vision & Screenshot Forensic Scanner Details

The newly integrated Screenshot Impersonation & Phishing Scanner allows users and analysts to identify threats from images without manually retyping obscure URLs or handles:

  • Multi-Format Ingestion: Drag-and-drop or file upload for PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WEBP. Supports direct system clipboard paste (Ctrl + V) after capturing a screenshot.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Engine: Extracts address bar URLs (including deceptive TLDs like .xyz, .co), social media handles (@threatlens_support_bot), and Android package names (com.threatlens.guard.mod).
  • Visual Spoofing Detection: Identifies cloned corporate Single Sign-On (SSO) login forms, counterfeit brand vector marks, emoji checkmarks used to fake verified status, and predatory permission requests.
  • Interactive Hotspot Pins: Displays numbered interactive pins directly over the screenshot image highlighting the exact location of detected fraud (Address Bar, Profile Bio, Download Link).
  • 1-Click Remediation: Instant export of formal legal Takedown Notices for domain registrars, hosting providers, or social platform trust & safety teams.
